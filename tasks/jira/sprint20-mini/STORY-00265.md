# STORY-00265: 网兜长度修复 — 网兜可触达所有星星

**Epic**: E-001
**Sprint**: Sprint 20-mini
**Sprint Goal**: 修复真机4个Blocker缺陷
**Points**: 2
**Owner**: Frontend Dev
**Status**: Done

## Description
As a player, I want to be able to catch all stars in a level so I can complete it.

## Expected Result
Net extends far enough to reach stars in the upper sky zone. Game is completable.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Net max length ≥ H * 0.75 (up from 0.55) — net head reaches well into the sky zone
- [ ] Stars placed at skyY0=SAFE_TOP+60 to skyY1=H*0.62 are all reachable by the net
- [ ] Net does not extend off the top of the screen (cap at ropeOriY - SAFE_TOP - 20)
- [ ] Game is playable: player can catch stars and complete a level
- [ ] Net visual still looks correct at the new length (bag renders at head position)

## Root Cause
_netMaxLen = H * 0.55. ropeOriY = H*0.82 - 60.
Net top reach = H*0.82 - 60 - H*0.55 = H*0.27 - 60.
On 667px screen: 0.27*667-60 = 120px from top.
Stars at skyY1=H*0.62 = 414px from top. Gap = 294px — net cannot reach stars.
Fix: increase _netMaxLen to H * 0.75 so net reaches to H*0.82-60-H*0.75 = H*0.07-60 ≈ -13px (top of screen).

## Notes
Also check star placement zone — if stars are placed above H*0.62, adjust skyY1 or netMaxLen accordingly.
