# STORY-00037: 场景背景地景结构

**Epic**: E-010 新迭代改进
**Sprint**: Sprint 10
**Sprint Goal**: 核心游戏体验升级
**Points**: 5
**Owner**: Frontend Dev
**Status**: Todo

## Description
As a player, I want game backgrounds to show a ground-sky composition (rolling hills or mountain silhouette at bottom, star sky above), giving a sense of being in a real New Zealand location.

## Expected Result
Each of the 6 scene groups has a distinct ground silhouette layer in the game canvas background. The sky occupies the upper ~75% of the canvas; the ground silhouette the lower ~25%. Stars and gameplay objects appear only in the sky zone.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] All 6 scene backgrounds show a ground silhouette at the bottom of the game canvas
- [ ] Scene 0 (特卡波湖·牧羊人小屋): rolling hills + lake reflection, church silhouette suggestion
- [ ] Scene 1 (库克山·山顶雪景): jagged snow-covered mountain peaks
- [ ] Scene 2 (特卡波湖·夏夜全景): wide rolling landscape + distant hills
- [ ] Scene 3 (库克山·山谷营地): valley walls on sides + camp light glow hint
- [ ] Scene 4 (特卡波湖·冬夜极光): low rolling hills, aurora bands in sky
- [ ] Scene 5 (库克山·破晓前深空): dramatic rocky peak silhouette, near-black
- [ ] Ground silhouettes are canvas-drawn (not img elements) — vector/procedural, not pixel art
- [ ] Gameplay zone (stars, debris) remains in upper 75% of canvas — stars do NOT spawn behind ground layer
- [ ] Zero console errors

## Notes
Approach: In engine.js `_drawBackground()`, after drawing sky gradient, draw ground silhouette using canvas Path2D per scene palette. Each silhouette defined as a series of bezier curves stored in scenes.js alongside the palette. The ground color should be very dark (darker than sky horizon) with a subtle blue-purple glow at the edge to suggest city/ambient light. No real image files needed — pure canvas drawing is sufficient and avoids asset dependencies. If real CC0 images are found that have better quality, they may be used as `drawImage` with `globalAlpha` overlay, but canvas drawing is the primary implementation.
