## Description
As a player, I want the gallery astrophotography images to load instantly when I open the gallery, without visible loading delay.

## Expected Result
Photos display immediately (from browser cache) when entering the gallery detail screen.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Photos are preloaded at app startup (not just when levels screen is visited)
- [ ] Image references stored in module-level array to prevent GC before load completes
- [ ] ESA Hubble CDN domain has preconnect + dns-prefetch hints in index.html
- [ ] Gallery photos display without loading spinner on second+ entry
