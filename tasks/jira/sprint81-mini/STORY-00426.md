## Description
As a player, I want to see a preview of the next level on the victory result card, so that I feel motivated to continue and know what challenge awaits.

## Expected Result
Below the buttons row on the victory card, a small preview line shows the next constellation's name, star count, and difficulty indicator — a teaser that increases anticipation for continuing.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] AC1: On victory card when `_levelIdx < 29`, a preview line appears below the 3-button row: "下一关: [nameZh] · [stars.length]颗星 · 难度[difficulty]" in `rgba(180,200,240,0.75)`, font size 10px, centered.
- [ ] AC2: A 4px colored dot (difficulty indicator) is drawn inline before "难度": green (diff 1-2), amber (diff 3), red (diff 4-5).
- [ ] AC3: On level 30 (`_levelIdx >= 29`), shows "全部关卡完成！" in gold (#ffd700) instead of next-level info.
- [ ] AC4: Line is positioned within card bottom padding area; if vertical space < 14px from card bottom, line is hidden rather than overlapping buttons.
- [ ] AC5: Preview correctly reads from `CONSTELLATIONS[_levelIdx + 1]` — constellation name, star count, and difficulty are accurate.
