# STORY-00424: 星星捕获视觉冲击升级 — 缩放涟漪+屏幕白闪

**Epic**: E-008 游戏体验精化
**Sprint**: Sprint 81-mini
**Sprint Goal**: 提升游戏核心手感 — 星星捕获视觉升级、首通/重玩文案区分、下一关预告、抓空惩罚、失败统计
**Points**: 3
**Owner**: Frontend Dev
**Status**: Done

## Description
As a player, I want catching a star to feel spectacular and impactful, so that every successful catch is satisfying and motivating.

## Expected Result
Each star catch produces a circular ring expanding outward from the catch point (300ms, radius 0→60px, alpha 0.6→0, tier-matched color). The caught star flashes white for 80ms before fading. Final-star catch and 3+ combo catches trigger a brief full-screen white flash.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] AC1: When a star is caught, a circular ring (stroke, not fill) expands outward from the catch point over 300ms (radius 0→60px), alpha fading 0.6→0, color matching the star's tier color. Ring drawn behind burst particles.
- [ ] AC2: The caught star flashes to full white (#ffffff, alpha 1.0) for 80ms before beginning its 250ms fade-out — creates a "pop" moment at the instant of capture.
- [ ] AC3: When catching the final star (triggers allcaught_burst), a full-screen white flash overlay (alpha 0.15, fading over 200ms) briefly pulses.
- [ ] AC4: When a combo of 3+ is achieved at any catch, the catch also triggers a white flash overlay (alpha 0.10, fading over 150ms) — subtler than the final-star flash.
- [ ] AC5: All new effects are dt-scaled (not frame-counted), do not leak memory, produce zero console errors.

## Dependencies
- None

## Notes
Implementation: `_catchRings` array — each ring: {x, y, r, maxR, alpha, color, elapsed}. Update in `_updateParticles`, draw in `_drawCatchRings` called after stars. `_screenFlashAlpha` float variable, decremented per dt, drawn as full-canvas rect. Per-star `_starFlash` map: starIdx → elapsed (tracks the 80ms white flash per star).
