# BUG-00261-STORY-00259: 发射轨迹粒子密度严重不足

**Priority**: Critical
**Sprint**: Sprint 19-mini
**Opened by**: QA
**Status**: Fixed

## Description
AC1 for STORY-00259 specifies "3-4 glowing white particles spawned per frame during net extension". The implementation spawns particles with a 60% random probability, resulting in ~0.6 particles per frame on average — approximately 6x below specification. The net launch feels significantly less impressive than designed.

## Steps to Reproduce
Launch the net. Observe the trail of white particles behind the net head during extension.

## Expected
3-4 white trail particles spawned per frame, creating a dense glowing trail.

## Actual
On average 0.6 particles per frame (60% random chance, 1 particle per spawn event). Trail appears sparse.

## Root Cause
`_updateNet` only calls the spawn block once per frame with `Math.random() < 0.6`. To achieve 3-4 particles/frame, need a loop spawning 3-4 particles unconditionally.

## Fix
Loop spawning 3-4 particles per frame during extension instead of a single probabilistic spawn.
