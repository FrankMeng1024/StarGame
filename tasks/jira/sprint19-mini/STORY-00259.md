# STORY-00259: 操作流程精修 — 发射手感 + 反馈完整

**Epic**: Mini 视觉精品化
**Sprint**: Sprint 19-mini
**Sprint Goal**: 视觉效果全面拉满
**Points**: 2
**Owner**: Frontend Dev
**Status**: Done

## Description
As a player, I want every action to feel responsive and satisfying — launching the net has a trail, catching a star bursts with gold particles, hitting debris shakes the screen, and I always know what to do next.

## Expected Result
Net launch spawns particle trail. Catch burst upgraded to 12 particles. Debris hit adds screen shake. Victory line-draw has subtle SFX. First launch hint upgraded to animated pulse.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Launch trail: 3-4 glowing white particles spawned per frame during net extension, fading over 15 frames
- [ ] Catch burst: 12 particles (6 gold + 3 white + 3 star-color), r=5, life=36 frames
- [ ] Debris hit: 6-frame screen shake ±3px on catch debris collision
- [ ] Screen shake: after 6 frames, ctx offset returns to (0,0) — no permanent shift
- [ ] Victory line-draw: each line drawn triggers sfx-catch.wav at volume 0.4 (or dedicated sfx-linedraw.wav if available)
- [ ] First-launch hint: shows pulsing circle at bottom of screen for 1.5s on first game session (in addition to existing text hint)
