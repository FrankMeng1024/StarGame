# BUG-00263-STORY-00258: 帽子星星字体14px应为12px

**Priority**: Medium
**Sprint**: Sprint 19-mini
**Opened by**: QA
**Status**: Fixed

## Description
Hat star decoration uses font='14px sans-serif', AC specifies 12px. The star on the hat is 17% larger than designed.

## Root Cause
Typo in _drawGirl hat section — 14px written instead of 12px.

## Fix
Changed `ctx.font = '14px sans-serif'` → `ctx.font = '12px sans-serif'` in _drawGirl hat section.
