# STORY-00087: 网兜和人物位置修复 — 网兜应在角色手持位置

**Epic**: E-018 用户反馈修复
**Sprint**: Sprint 18
**Sprint Goal**: 8项用户反馈修复
**Points**: 3
**Owner**: Frontend Dev
**Status**: Todo

## Description
As a player, I want the net to appear held in the character's hand, so that the visual relationship between net and character feels natural.

## Expected Result
When idle (swinging), the net hoop hangs from the pole tip at the character's hand position, not detached/offset from the character.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Net rope originates from the pole tip (which extends from the character's hand)
- [ ] When idle, net hoop is visible at the pole tip end
- [ ] During extend/retract, net movement starts from the pole tip
- [ ] No visual gap between character hand and net attachment point

## Notes
The pole is already drawn from charX, cy-22 to _poleTop(). The net _drawNet() needs to render at the pole top when at rest.
