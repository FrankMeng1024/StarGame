# STORY-00094: 星星视觉半径放大至当前2.5倍

**Epic**: E-018 用户反馈修复
**Sprint**: Sprint 18
**Sprint Goal**: 8项用户反馈修复
**Points**: 2
**Owner**: Frontend Dev
**Status**: Todo

## Description
As a player, I want the stars in the game to be visually larger (2.5x current size) so they are more prominent against the night sky.

## Expected Result
Stars appear 2.5x larger than current. The overall star radius formula and caps are scaled proportionally.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Star base radius multiplier changed from ×0.8 (1.2 × 2/3) to ×2.5
- [ ] MAX_STAR_R increased proportionally (from 9 to ~22)
- [ ] MIN_STAR_R remains at 3
- [ ] Visual glow and twinkle effects scale with the new radius
- [ ] Stars are clearly more prominent but not overwhelmingly large
- [ ] Caught star dim dots scale proportionally
