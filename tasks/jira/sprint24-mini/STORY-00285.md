As a player, I want the girl character to look more attractive and detailed, matching the quality of the web SVG version.

- [ ] Hair: purple highlight arc (#553399) over top hair cap for shine effect
- [ ] Dress hem: width ±32px at base (was ±26px) — fuller princess silhouette
- [ ] Hat brim: rx=26 (was 22), crown top at y=-114 (was -108)
- [ ] Hat star: drawn as 5-point polygon (ctx.beginPath with computed star vertices), not emoji text
- [ ] Character anchor point unchanged: center bottom at (_poleX, _poleY)
