# STORY-00286: 网兜物理完整化 — 速度+垃圾拖拽

**Epic**: 用户体验修复
**Sprint**: Sprint 24-mini
**Sprint Goal**: 用户反馈8项修复
**Points**: 2
**Owner**: Frontend Dev
**Status**: Done

## Description
As a player, I want the net to feel physically realistic — moving at a natural speed, and visibly dragging debris back when caught instead of mysteriously repelling it.

## Expected Result
Net extends at a comfortable speed (about 1.5s full traversal). When debris is caught, it is visibly dragged back with the net, feeling heavy and physical.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] NET_SPEED reduced from 5 to 4 px/frame (240px/s at 60fps)
- [ ] During retract with _caughtDebris, the debris is drawn at the net head position (not its original position)
- [ ] Retract speed with debris: NET_SPEED * 0.20 (was 0.30) — even slower, heavier feel
- [ ] After retract completes with _caughtDebris: -1s time penalty applied, _caughtDebris cleared
- [ ] Star catch behavior unchanged (stars are captured normally)

## Dependencies
None

## Notes
The visual drag is the key user-facing change — debris must visibly follow the net head during retract. Current code may set _caughtDebris but never renders it at net position.
