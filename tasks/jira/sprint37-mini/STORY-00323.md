## Description
As a player, I want stars to light up one by one in the moment of victory (before the lines connect), just like in the HTML web version, so it feels satisfying and sequential.

## Expected Result
Victory sequence: all stars dim → each constellation star flashes individually (scale pulse 1→2→1, glow burst) in order → then gold lines connect them. Not just lines appearing.
