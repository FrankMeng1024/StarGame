## Description
As a player, I want the net to swing wider so I can reach stars near the edges of the screen, so that all stars in a level are catchable.

## Expected Result
The net swings to ±80° from vertical, covering stars at the horizontal extremes of the play field (e.g. Scorpius level stars at x=0.78).

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] `swingMax` set to ±80° (was ±60°)
- [ ] Stars at x~0.78 in level 3 (Scorpius) are reachable by the net
- [ ] No visual clipping or breakage at extreme angles

## Dependencies
- None

CR-062: Changed `this.swingMax = Math.PI * 60 / 180` to `Math.PI * 80 / 180` in engine.js.
