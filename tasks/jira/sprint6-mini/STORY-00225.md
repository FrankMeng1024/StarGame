## Description
As a player, I want a satisfying victory celebration when I catch all stars so that completing a constellation feels rewarding and memorable.

## Expected Result
When all stars are caught:
1. Brief particle celebration (gold star-rain, 1.5 seconds)
2. Constellation line animation: lines draw one-by-one connecting star positions (0.8 seconds total)
3. Result overlay appears with full lore text (up to 200 chars, word-wrapped, scrollable if longer)
