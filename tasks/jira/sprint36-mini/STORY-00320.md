## Description
As a player, I want the item shop to look sophisticated and match the starry theme, not cartoonish with colorful emojis.

## Expected Result
Shop cards use deep blue-purple gradients with gold accents, Canvas-drawn geometric icons instead of emoji, refined type badges.

- [ ] 每种道具有独特Canvas绘制图标（不用emoji），颜色：金色/紫色调，带圆形光晕背景

- [ ] 购买按钮：渐变 `#5533aa` → `#8855ee`
- [ ] 截图验证：shop截图显示精致卡片风格，无emoji图标
- [ ] 截图亮度 > 10
