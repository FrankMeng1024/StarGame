# STORY-00075: 失败界面布局修复

**Epic**: E-017 游戏手感修复
**Sprint**: Sprint 17
**Sprint Goal**: 游戏手感全面修复
**Points**: 3
**Owner**: Frontend Dev
**Status**: Todo

## Description
As a player who fails a level, I want the fail screen to look as polished and spacious as the complete screen, not cramped and squished.

## Expected Result
Fail screen has proper vertical spacing, uses the full screen height, and includes a brief encouragement message or constellation silhouette to fill the lower area. Elements are not crammed together.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Fail screen title "⏰ 时间到了！" is readable with adequate top margin
- [ ] Stats row (caught/total, 0秒, 0金币) is clearly spaced — not compressed against title
- [ ] Lower portion of screen shows encouragement text (e.g. "再试一次，星座等着你！") or a dimmed constellation silhouette — not empty dead space
- [ ] Retry button is visually prominent and not squeezed to the bottom
- [ ] "返回选关" button is present and styled the same as the fail screen retry CTA
- [ ] Fail screen height matches complete screen — no content overflow or clipping
- [ ] Screenshot comparison: fail screen and complete screen are both polished at same viewport
- [ ] Zero console errors

## Notes
VU Sprint 16 observation: "Fail screen has excessive empty dead space in lower 70% of screen — feels unfinished compared to the polished complete screen." The photo/lore is intentionally hidden on fail (player hasn't earned story). Fill the space with encouragement text or a faint constellation silhouette (canvas-drawn, same as mini canvas on complete screen but larger and dimmer).
