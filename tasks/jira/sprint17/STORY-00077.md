# STORY-00077: 背景音乐重制

**Epic**: E-017 游戏手感修复
**Sprint**: Sprint 17
**Sprint Goal**: 游戏手感全面修复
**Points**: 3
**Owner**: Frontend Dev
**Status**: Todo

## Description
As a player, I want the background music to feel like a single coherent ethereal/space ambient piece, not two tracks playing simultaneously.

## Expected Result
A single unified ambient audio track that feels soft, spacious, and ethereal — like stargazing music. No distinct "melody on top of chords" separation. All layers blend into one atmospheric sound.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Background music has no audible "two layer" separation — all sound elements blend into a single atmospheric texture
- [ ] Music is distinctly softer and more spacious than the current version
- [ ] Music loops seamlessly without clicks or volume jumps
- [ ] Mute toggle still works (masterGain still controls all audio)
- [ ] Sound effects (catch, debris, reveal, countdown) are unchanged
- [ ] No console errors during gameplay

## Notes
Root cause of "two merged tracks" feel: current implementation schedules chord oscillators (CHORD_VOL=0.04) AND melody triangle wave simultaneously at similar volumes, with both starting abruptly every beat. This creates two audible melodic streams competing.

Fix approach — single-layer ambient drone pad:
1. Remove the separate chord + melody + twinkle scheduling loop
2. Replace with: slow-attack sine-wave drone on root note (A3=220Hz) with detuned octave (A4=440Hz, slight detune ±2 cents) — creates a beating/shimmer effect without two separate lines
3. Add slow-evolving BiquadFilter (lowpass, frequency sweeping 400→1200Hz over 8 seconds and back) — gives movement without melodic structure
4. Add sparse triangle arpeggio notes (random intervals 2-5s, same pentatonic scale, very low volume 0.01-0.015, long sustain 1.2s) — these should be isolated, not stacked
5. Optional: DelayNode (0.3s delay, 0.35 feedback) on arpeggio only for space/depth feel
6. All oscillators go through a single _musicGain node (separate from masterGain) at volume 0.08 total

Key: the "ethereal" quality comes from slow attack/release on all notes (no staccato), detuning, and low-pass filter movement — not from harmonic density.
