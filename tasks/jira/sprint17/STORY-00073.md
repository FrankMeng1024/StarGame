# STORY-00073: 网兜静止可见

**Epic**: E-017 游戏手感修复
**Sprint**: Sprint 17
**Sprint Goal**: 游戏手感全面修复
**Points**: 2
**Owner**: Frontend Dev
**Status**: Todo

## Description
As a player, I want to see the net at all times, so that I know its current position before launching.

## Expected Result
The net hoop and bag are visible at the pole tip while swinging, not just when Space is pressed to extend.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Net (hoop + mesh) is visible at all times during gameplay, including when `netState === 'swing'`
- [ ] When idle/swinging, net renders at the pole tip with a small fixed extension (enough to draw the hoop visually)
- [ ] When Space is pressed, net extends normally from the visible idle position
- [ ] Net catch/retract states unchanged — no regression on catch animation
- [ ] Zero console errors during gameplay

## Notes
Root cause: `_drawNet()` returns early at `if (len < 2)` when `netPos === 0` and `netState === 'swing'` (tip === top, len = 0). Fix: when netState === 'swing', use a fixed small display offset (e.g. 32px) so net hoop renders at idle position. The pole+rope rendering block also needs to fire at swing state.
