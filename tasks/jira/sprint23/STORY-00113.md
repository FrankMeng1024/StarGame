# STORY-00113: 网绳发射起点修复 — 从摆动时的手部位置出发

**Epic**: E-010 游戏体验
**Sprint**: Sprint 23
**Sprint Goal**: 2项游戏体验修复
**Points**: 2
**Owner**: Frontend Dev
**Status**: Done

## Description
As a player, when I press Space to throw the net, I want the rope to depart from the same position where it was visually swinging, not suddenly jump to the top of the character's head.

## Expected Result
When net is in swing state, the rope/net visually originates from the idle hand position (cx+13, cy-17). When Space is pressed, the rope extends from that same idle position — no visual jump or flicker to a different anchor point.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] During `netState === 'swing'`: hand position is idle `(cx+13, cy-17)` with gentle bob — rope and net originate from here
- [ ] At the moment Space is pressed (`netState` transitions to `extend`): rope departs from `(cx+13, cy-17)` — same position as swing state, no jump
- [ ] During `netState === 'extend'` and `'retract'`: hand position stays at `(cx+13, cy-17)` — consistent throughout the full flight cycle
- [ ] No sudden 61px vertical jump in the rope's anchor point at any state transition
- [ ] Character throw animation (frame 2 sprite) still plays visually, but the **rope anchor** remains at the idle hand position

## Root Cause
Sprint 22 STORY-00111 fixed the retract flicker by holding `(cx+27, cy-78)` for the full extend+retract cycle. However this introduced a new jump: at the moment Space is pressed, the hand snaps from `(cx+13, cy-17)` (swing/idle) to `(cx+27, cy-78)` (throw-frame). User sees rope instantly jump ~61px upward to near head-top at launch.

## Fix
Change `_updateHandPos()` to use idle hand position `(cx+13, cy-17)` for ALL states (swing, extend, retract). The throw-frame sprite animation shows the character raising their arm visually, but the rope anchor point should stay at the idle hand coordinates throughout. This makes the rope appear to launch from where it was swinging.
