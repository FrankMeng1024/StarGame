# BUG-00004: "难度" label barely legible at 9px/60% opacity

**Priority**: Medium
**Sprint**: Backlog
**Opened by**: UX (Sprint 13 Review)
**Status**: Open

## Description
The "难度" label on level cards uses 9px font size at 60% opacity. On unlocked cards (with lighter backgrounds), the label blends into the card background and may be missed by first-time users who do not understand what the color bar represents without the label.

## Evidence
docs/ux/sprint13-evidence/ux-A3-card-closeup.png

## Fix Options
- Increase font size to 10-11px
- Increase opacity to 80%+
- Or add a small icon (e.g. ⚔) before the label as a secondary affordance
